A phone app records high-frame-rate video of a swing with one or two smartphone cameras. A custom YOLO-based pose model locates keypoints on the bat in each frame, and an XGBoost model turns those trajectories into bat speed, attack angle, and time to contact, shown to the hitter within seconds of the swing. Assigned to Driveline Baseball; co-invented with Clayton Thompson.
